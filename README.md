<h1>📊 Project Title</h1>
<h1>Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis</h1>
<hr>
<h1>📋 Project Overview</h1>
<p>This project analyzes marketing campaign performance to identify channels, campaigns, and customer segments generates the highest conversions, revenue and return on advertising spend (ROAS)</p>
<p>Using campaign, customer and performance data, the analysis evaluates marketing spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue, CTR, conversion rate, and ROAS, to uncover opportunities for improving campaign effectiveness and optimizing marketing budget.</p>
<hr>
<h1>🎯 Business Problem</h1> 
<p>The marketing team is investing heavily across multiple digital channels but lacks a clear understanding of which campaigns and channels are delivering the best business results.</p>
<p>With increasing marketing spend, the company needs to identify high-performing campaigns, profitable channels, valuable customer segments, and inefficient marketing investment. The analysis aims to provide data-driven recommendations for improving campaign performance, increasing conversions, and optimizing marketing budget allocation.</p>
<h2>Business Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Determine which marketing channels generate the strongest traffic, engagement, conversions, revenue, and ROAS?</li>
  <li>Identify the best and worst-performing campaigns based on spending, conversions, revenue and return on advertising spend.</li>
  <li>Analyze how different customer segments, demographics and audience respond to marketing campaigns.</li>
  <li>Determine which channels, audiences and customer segments should receive greater marketing attention.</li>
</ul>

<h1>💡 Key Insights</h1>
<ol>
  <li>The campaigns generated approximately 8.83B in revenue from 1.05B in marketing spending, resulting in an overall ROAS of 8.40x.</li>
  <li>LinkedIn generated the highest channel revenue and conversions, contributing approximately 1.46B in revenue and 1.88B conversions, followed by Meta Ads and Google Ads.</li>
  <li>New Customers were the largest revenue-contributing segment, generating approximately 3.48B (39%) of the total revenue, followed by Loyal Customers 2.71B (31%).</li>
  <li>Revenue varied considerably across cities, with Kolkata generating the highest revenue (1.31B), followed by Pune (1.16B) and Hyderabad (1.12B) indicating difference in regional campaign performance.</li>
  <li>Customer conversion rates were highly consistent across segments and devices, remaining around 10.47%–10.50%, suggesting that conversion performance is relatively stable across these groups.</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h1>🚀 Recommendations</h1>
<ol>
  <li>Continue investing in channels such as LinkedIn, Meta Ads, and Google Ads while monitoring their ROAS and conversion efficiency before increasing budgets.</li>
  <li>Review campaigns appearing in the bottom-performing ROAS group and investigate their targeting, creative strategy, audience selection and spending efficiency.</li>
  <li>Since New Customer contribute the largest share of revenue, maintain strong acquisition campaigns while using retargeting strategies to convert new customers into returning and loyal customers.</li>
  <li>Analyze the targeting channel, audience and campaign objectives of the highest-performing campaigns and apply those patterns to future campaign.</li>
  <li>Investigate high-performing cities such as Kolkata, Pune and Hyderabad and Consider localized campaigns or targeted promotions to further improve regional performance.</li>
</ol>
